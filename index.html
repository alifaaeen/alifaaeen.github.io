ABOUT ME

I’m a freelance professional based in Islamabad, Pakistan 🇵🇰, with over three years of experience in automating workflows and creating tailored applications using Excel and Google Sheets.

My Excel journey started way back in 2022, when I read a quote that stayed with me: "The economy of this world runs on two things—petrol and Excel." It was a straightforward quote, but it made me realize the potential of Excel, and since then, I committed myself to learning it. Over the years, I’ve helped countless clients leverage Excel to optimize their workflows, elevate their business processes, and take their operations to new heights.

I’ve worked on platforms like Upwork and Fiverr, completing more than 500 projects, all with five-star ratings. Each project taught me something new, but the satisfaction of helping clients transform their work through automation is what truly drives me.

Aside from my expertise in Excel, I’m also a Chartered Accountant student, on my way to becoming a Chartered Accountant, InshaAllah. This background has given me a solid understanding of accounting and tax, which has been invaluable as I’ve worked with some of Pakistan’s largest companies, helping them doing their of taxes and accounting.

With all that experience under my belt, I'm here to assist you in reaching your objectives. We can work together to streamline your processes and bring your business to the next level.
